# AcmeTech Internal Processes

Standard operating procedures for day-to-day operations at AcmeTech.

## Expenses and reimbursements

- Expense reports are submitted through the HR portal and approved within five business days.
- Meals are reimbursable up to 60 USD per day when traveling.
- Purchases above 5,000 USD require written manager approval before the order is placed.

## Equipment provisioning

- New laptops are shipped within three business days of a hiring manager's request.
- A standard developer setup includes 32GB of RAM, a 1TB SSD, and two external monitors.
- Damaged equipment is returned to the IT depot; employees are not personally liable.

## Onboarding

- Day one includes badge pickup, laptop setup, and an allocated onboarding buddy.
- New hires gain production access only after the security review clears.
- The 30-day onboarding checklist is tracked in the HR portal.

## Offboarding

- Access is revoked on the employee's last working day before 5 PM.
- Handover notes must be reviewed by the team lead before the departure date.
