# AcmeTech Customer Support FAQ

Frequently asked questions collected from real support tickets.

## Account and billing

- Q: How do I upgrade my plan? A: Log in to the portal, open Billing, and select the new plan. Changes take effect immediately.
- Q: Do you offer refunds? A: Pro-rated refunds are available within 14 days of renewal.
- Q: Where can I see my invoice? A: Invoices are downloadable from the Billing page under Invoices.

## Product usage

- Q: How many team seats are included? A: The Starter plan includes five seats; the Pro plan includes twenty.
- Q: Can I export my dashboards? A: Yes. Dashboard exports are available as PDF or CSV.
- Q: Is there an API rate limit? A: The public API allows 1,000 requests per minute on paid plans.

## Troubleshooting

- Q: My data source keeps disconnecting. A: Confirm your firewall allows outbound HTTPS, then reconnect in Settings.
- Q: Reports arrive empty. A: Empty reports are usually caused by a timezone mismatch between the workspace and the report schedule.

## Security questions

- Q: Do you support single sign-on? A: Yes, SSO is enabled on all paid plans.
- Q: Where is my data hosted? A: Data is hosted in the same region as your workspace unless otherwise configured.
